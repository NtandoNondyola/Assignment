Organisation Overview

Name: Ntando Agriculture

Ntando Agriculture is a small-to-medium, Black-owned agribusiness based in Bloemfontein, South Africa. Founded in 2025 by Ntando Nonjola, the company specializes in ethical and sustainable livestock farming. The farm raises cattle, goats, sheep, and chickens for meat, milk, and egg production, supplying high-quality products to local markets and businesses.

Mission

To promote food security and rural development by providing ethically raised, organic livestock products while empowering local communities.

Vision

To become a leader in sustainable livestock farming in South Africa, creating a model for humane animal care and community-supported agriculture.

Target Audience

  • Local consumers
  • Butcheries and grocery stores
  • Restaurants and hotels
  • School and hospital kitchens
  • Agriculture investors and partners

Website Goals and Objectives

Goals

  • Showcase the farm’s livestock and ethical farming practices.
  • Educate the public on animal welfare and sustainability.
  • Create an online space for product orders and partnership inquiries.
  • Attract funding and collaborations for rural development.
